PLAY WITHOUT BROWSER BARS

On iPhone and iPad, use a Home Screen web app when browser fullscreen is unavailable. This button cannot hide the browser bars directly.

  1. Open your browser’s Share menu (in Safari, tap More, then Share).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen (under View More if needed). Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.
  3. Open nitro.car from your Home Screen.

If your browser does not offer Add to Home Screen, open this same address in Safari and follow these steps. iOS may still show its status bar.
